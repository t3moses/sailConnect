Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

Everything you need to know about Sail Friday

Background

What is the aim of the Nepean Sailing Club Sail Friday program?

We're here to serve the cruising community at Nepean Sailing Club, whether you own a boat or you don't (yet!).

How can I tell if this program is a good fit for me?

Crew members join to build and practice their sailing skills. If you're after something different, check out the other programs at Nepean Sailing Club.

How does the Sail Friday program operate?

The program runs on a distributed model. The info lives with the people in it, and you can find it on the Sail Friday network on WhatsApp. You can join the network when you register.

You manage your own participation through the program website.

Getting Started

Do I need to re-register every year?

Yep! We wipe the database clean at the end of each season, so everyone (including returning sailors) needs to sign up fresh for the new year.

I have an account with Nepean Sailing Club. Do I need to re-register with the Sail Friday program?

'Fraid so! You'll need to create a separate account for the Sail Friday program.

How is my personal information handled?

Your personal info is processed and/or stored by Nepean Sailing Club, Amazon Web Services and Mailjet. Here's where you can read their privacy policies:

  • NSC - Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Web Services Privacy Policy
  • Mailjet Privacy Policy

How much does it cost?

Non-members can sail a couple of times for free. To keep going, you'll need a Crewing Membership at Nepean Sailing Club. Bonus: it gives you priority when spots are tight!

Events & Registration

I registered but can't make it—what now?

No worries! Just update your availability on the website before 10:00 AM on event day. That way you won't get hit with a no-show.

The system will be unavailable for updates after 10:00 AM.

I freed up and can now join an event—how?

Sweet! Update your availability on the website. The system will auto-add you to the event or the waitlist depending on space.

What happens if I ghost an event?

Not cool! Your skipper adjusted their own plans so you'd get a chance to sail on their boat. Their commitment is crucial to the program's future, and that's not something we're going to put at risk. Plus, when there's a waitlist, you've taken up a spot someone else could've used. No-show and you'll get flagged, bumped down in priority, and might get left out of future events. Make a habit of it and you could get booted from the program altogether.

When's the registration deadline?

10:00 AM on event day. After that, the calendar freezes and any cancellation counts as a no-show.

How do I know if bad weather cancels an event?

The skippers will confer in the morning. They decide if it's safe to sail, and you'll get an email right away. We only cancel for serious stuff—lightning, crazy winds, or nasty air quality.

Can I show up late or leave early?

Nope! You gotta commit to the whole thing—arrive by 12:45 PM and stay for the full 3-4 hour sail. It's only fair to your boat mates and the skipper.

Assignments & Matching

Why does my boat keep changing?

The system recalculates every time someone updates their availability. It's making sure crews are balanced across all boats. Your assignment might bounce around until the 10:00 AM deadline—totally normal!

Can I sail with my partner/friend?

Sorry, no! We're all about mixing it up and meeting new people. The whole point is building community, not just hanging with people you already know.

Can I request a specific boat?

Nope! The auto system keeps things fair by mixing everyone up. This way you get to experience different boats and learn from various skippers. Variety is the spice of sailing!

Why am I always on the waitlist?

Few reasons this might happen:

  • Not an NSC member yet (members get priority)
  • Didn't enter a valid membership number when registering for the program
  • Previous no-shows tanking your priority
  • Signing up less than a week before the event
  • Event is just super popular

Pro tip: Become a member and register early!

How do I know if I get off the waitlist?

Check the events page—it updates in real-time when spots open up. Calendar freezes at 10:00 AM on event day.

I'm a boat owner, but I don't mind crewing if there's ever a shortage. What should I do?

Register as a boat owner and check the box saying you can crew on another boat if needed. Whenever you're available, your boat joins the flotilla, unless there aren't enough crew for the other boats. In that case, you'll get a crew spot on one of those boats.

Sail Friday Network

What is the Sail Friday network on WhatsApp?

This is a WhatsApp group and THE place to go for all things Sail Friday. Ask your questions about the program, the club, boats and sailing in general!

Lots of folks come and go each year, so we delete the group at the start of every season.

What to Expect

What should I bring?

Here's the essentials list:

  • Closed-toe, non-slip shoes (MUST have!)
  • Hat, sunscreen, and sunglasses
  • Water bottle
  • Clothes for the weather (layers are smart)
  • PFD if you have one (we'll hook you up if not)
  • Good vibes!

Where do we meet?

Blue tent on the west lawn at 12:45 PM. BE ON TIME!

Do I need sailing experience?

Absolutely not! We welcome everyone from total newbies to old salts. The whole point is learning and sharing knowledge across all skill levels.

How long are the sails?

Each cruise is 3-4 hours, then we hang out on the club deck afterward.
